Changelog
=========

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

- The garage banner and its hover card, in the banner row shared with Campaign Tracker. The banner counts down to the end of the active series, or to the first end of an open series, in the format of Premium Time.
- Warnings for a vehicle below the Crucible tiers, and for vehicles still in a battle when a series could start.
- The battle panel, with a bar, the figures and a time-limit countdown for each condition of the current mission. Hold Ctrl to drag it. The mod keeps the position in `config.json`.
- The hover card and the battle panel show "OR" between the sets of a mission with a choice of condition sets.
- A mode of the banner and the card for an active series. The banner shows the shields left out of all shields below its head. The card shows the series alone: its name and time left, one icon for each shield, and every mission of the series.
- The battle panel counts the High Caliber and Top Gun awards.
- The mod reads the active series from the account tokens. Before, the banner could show no active series after a login or a battle. The game sets its own value only when the lobby has loaded.
- Settings for the garage banner, the battle panel and the side of the banner, in Mod Menu or in `config.json`.
